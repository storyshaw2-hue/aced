inside one run

The game and the studying are the same loop.

CONVICTION
  • Before banking a hand, bet LOW / MED / HIGH.

YOUR NOTES STAY YOURS — HERE'S EXACTLY WHAT THAT MEANS.

NOTES PROCESSING

Pasted text, PDFs, and Word files are parsed entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste or upload is sent to a server, and nothing is used to train any model.

STORAGE & RETENTION

The deck the parser builds is saved on your own device in local browser storage. Optional email sign-in only syncs progress metadata (XP, streaks, deck IDs) — never your note contents.

CONTROL & NO STREAK-GUILT

Delete a deck or clear everything from the library at any time. No punishment for missing a day — streaks celebrate consistency, not shame.

Bigger uploads and optional AI deck-generation are coming later as a paid upgrade. When they land, we'll spell out exactly what leaves the browser and let you opt in per deck. The core game stays free.
