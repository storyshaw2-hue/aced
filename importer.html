We parse your answers, not invent them. Use question/answer pairs or a structured file. PDFs and unstructured paragraphs need to be turned into questions first.

Supported formats and a JSON example

Text: one Term: definition per line, or alternating Q: and A: lines. CSV/TSV: question, answer, topic, explanation columns. The first two columns are required; a header is optional.

JSON: a questions array containing q, answer, and optional source and explain. For multiple choice, add 2 to 8 choices and use a zero-based answer index. Front/back flashcard arrays work too.

{"name":"My pack","questions":[{"q":"What powers a run?","answer":"Your study questions","source":"Getting started"}]}

Check the answers before you play. Cards without answer choices use self-rated recall; multiple-choice cards are graded automatically.
