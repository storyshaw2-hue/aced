Questions

Is this a free trial?

No. There is no trial, no tier, and no checkout anywhere in ACED. What you can play today is everything.

Then why does a pricing page exist?

Because people look for one before they invest time in a study tool. This is the answer: it costs nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved in your browser. Optional sign-in only exists to sync the same progress between your phone and your laptop.

Will this help me learn faster?

ACED gives you a better way to practice and to see where you actually stand — real questions, honest self-scoring, and material that comes back the moment you forget it. The work is still yours. Anyone promising a shortcut isn't being straight with you.
