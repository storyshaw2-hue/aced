Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION
  • You can play ACED without an account. Your packs, notes and progress stay in your browser.
  • If you choose to sign in, we store your email address and a copy of your study progress so it can follow you between devices.
  • If you ask for launch updates, we store the email address you submit.
  • No ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or rent your information.
  • To have your data deleted, email privacy@acedhq.com.

Who runs ACED

ACED (acedhq.com) is run by Story Shaw, an individual based in the United States. “We” in this policy means ACED. Questions about this policy go to privacy@acedhq.com.

What stays in your browser

Without an account, ACED keeps its data in your browser’s local storage on your device. That includes:

  • packs you build or import, and any draft notes you paste in;
  • your progress: review history, mastery, streaks, scores, unlocks and settings;
  • a short log of recent in-app activity, such as runs started and reviews completed.

Pasted text and PDF or Word files are read in your browser. ACED does not upload your original files. Clearing your browser’s site data for acedhq.com removes everything stored there. ACED does not set cookies.

Share links and exports contain the material you choose to share. Anyone you send one to can read it.

What we collect if you choose to

Signing in

Sign-in is optional. If you sign in, we collect your email address and send it a one-time sign-in link. We store:

  • your email address, an account ID and the date the account was created;
  • a scrambled (hashed) copy of each sign-in link, kept with your email address until the link is used or expires. Links expire after 15 minutes and work once;
  • when you sign out, a record that the session was ended, so it cannot be reused.

Your browser keeps a sign-in session for up to 60 days. That session includes your email address and is stored in your browser’s local storage until you sign out.

Syncing your progress

While you are signed in, ACED sends a copy of your progress for each pack to our server and merges it with what is already saved, so another device can pick it up. That copy contains:

  • review history, mastery, confidence ratings, streaks, daily results, scores, unlocks and settings;
  • question IDs and topic or source labels. For packs you made yourself, those labels can contain text you wrote;
  • the recent in-app activity log described above;
  • if an older version of ACED saved a launch-update email address in your browser, that address.

The full text of your packs and your draft notes are not part of sync.

From synced progress we also keep a few summary figures per pack (best streak, readiness and best mock score). These stay private unless you choose a public name (a handle) in the account menu. If you do, your handle and those figures become visible to anyone. Your email address is never shown. Choosing “Hide me” in the account menu removes your handle from public view.

Launch updates

If you submit the “notify me” form, we store your email address, which page you submitted it from and the time. We use it only to send updates about ACED features and study packs.

Contacting us

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

What is collected automatically

  • Hosting logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type, the page or address requested, and the time. We use these to keep the service running and to investigate problems.
  • Abuse limits. Our server uses your IP address briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one connection can make. It is not saved to our database.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google your IP address and the address of the page you are on.
  • PDF and Word import. The classic importer downloads two code libraries from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) when you import a PDF or Word file. Cloudflare receives your IP address when that happens. The file itself is not sent.

Our own server logs for sign-in record only a random request ID, the type of request and its outcome. They do not contain your email address or sign-in link.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not take payments. ACED is free, and we hold no card or billing details.
  • We do not use your study material to train AI models.

Who handles data for us

We use a small number of companies to run ACED. They process data on our behalf, under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Vercel hosts the website and the sign-in and sync service. It handles request details (including IP address) and the data passing through the service.
  • Neon (a Databricks company) runs our database. It holds account email addresses, synced progress and launch-update addresses.
  • Resend sends sign-in emails. It handles your email address and the sign-in email.
  • Google Fonts serves fonts. It receives your IP address and the page address.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) serves the import libraries. It receives your IP address, only when you import a PDF or Word file in the classic importer.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect ACED and the people who use it from fraud or abuse. If ACED is ever transferred to a new owner, your information would move with it under this policy, and we would post a notice here first.

Where data is processed

ACED is operated from the United States, and the companies above process data in the United States and possibly other countries. If you use ACED from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Account and synced progress: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Launch-update email addresses: until you ask to be removed.
  • Sign-in links: removed when used, or cleared after they expire.
  • Ended sessions: until the session would have expired anyway (up to 60 days).
  • Hosting logs and database backups: kept by our providers for a limited time under their own schedules. Copies can remain there for a short period after we delete something.

Your choices

  • Play without an account. Everything in ACED works without signing in.
  • Sign out. Signing out ends the session in that browser and stops sync from it. “Sign out everywhere” in the account menu ends your sessions on every device. Progress already synced stays on our server.
  • Stay off the leaderboard. You are not listed unless you pick a handle, and you can hide it again at any time in the account menu.
  • Delete a pack. Deleting a pack removes that pack’s data in the browser you are using. It does not remove copies on other devices or on our server.
  • Ask us. Email privacy@acedhq.com from the address in question to get a copy of what we hold, correct it, delete your account and synced progress, or be removed from launch updates. There is no self-service delete button yet. We will respond within 30 days and may need to confirm that the address is yours.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as the right to complain to a data protection authority. We will honor valid requests wherever you are.

Security

Connections to ACED and between our server and database are encrypted. Sign-in uses one-time links rather than passwords, and we store only scrambled copies of those links. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission. To report a security problem, email security@acedhq.com.

Children

ACED is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ACED handles your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
